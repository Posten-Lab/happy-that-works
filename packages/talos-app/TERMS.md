# Talos terms — deployment draft

Production service terms require the Talos operator's identity, contact details, supported services, billing terms if applicable, and approved policy links. Configure `EXPO_PUBLIC_TALOS_TERMS_URL` after those terms are approved.

The repository's [MIT license](../../LICENSE) governs the licensed source code. It does not define the terms of an independently operated Talos service. Connected providers retain their own terms.

The inherited service terms are retained in [the source archive](../../docs/rebrand/archive/upstream-terms.md) for provenance and are not presented as Talos service terms.
