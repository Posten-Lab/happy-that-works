# Talos privacy policy — deployment draft

A Talos production privacy policy has not been published. Configure `EXPO_PUBLIC_TALOS_PRIVACY_URL` only after the deployment operator has approved its policy.

The policy must describe the actual deployment: its operator and contact address, relay and object storage, encrypted content and visible metadata, retention and deletion, push delivery, optional analytics, subscriptions, voice services, and connected AI providers. Enable and document only services configured for Talos.

The inherited policy is retained in [the source archive](docs/rebrand/archive/upstream-privacy.md) for provenance. It does not describe or govern a Talos deployment. No upstream service promises, retention periods, or compliance claims are adopted by renaming the application.
